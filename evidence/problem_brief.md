# SignalMap — Problem Brief

## Status
Initial hypothesis. User research has not yet been conducted.

## Problem statement
Product teams collect feedback from app reviews, surveys and support channels. The information can be repetitive and fragmented, making it difficult to identify recurring issues, understand changes over time and connect prioritisation decisions to original user evidence.

## Target user
Primary hypothesis: Product Manager at a digital-product team who reviews customer feedback and helps prioritise product improvements.

## Jobs to be done
- When feedback arrives from multiple sources, organise it into meaningful themes.
- When an issue appears to be increasing, inspect the evidence and context.
- When comparing issues, understand frequency, severity signals, confidence and effort assumptions.

## Product hypothesis
If product managers can explore semantically grouped feedback, inspect source reviews and compare trends, they may identify and assess product opportunities more efficiently than through manual spreadsheet review.

## Research status
No interviews or usability tests are represented as completed in this template. Add participant type, date, consent, observed workflow, direct evidence and synthesis only after conducting research.

## Open questions
1. Which part of feedback analysis is most time-consuming?
2. What tools are currently used?
3. What makes a theme trustworthy?
4. What evidence is needed before prioritising an issue?
5. How should teams account for app popularity and feedback-source bias?
